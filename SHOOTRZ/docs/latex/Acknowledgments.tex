We would like to express our sincere gratitude to all those who contributed to the development and completion of this project.

First and foremost, our deepest thanks go to our supervisor, \textbf{Dr.\ Walaa Hassan}, whose guidance, technical insight, and constructive feedback shaped both the direction and the rigour of this work. Her willingness to engage with difficult engineering questions and to challenge us to think carefully about design decisions was invaluable throughout every stage of the project.

We extend our gratitude to the faculty of the \textbf{School of Computing \& Digital Technology at ESLSCA University} for providing the academic environment, resources, and encouragement that made this project possible. The curriculum in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science gave us the theoretical foundation from which SHOOTRZ grew.

We thank the broader open-source community whose work underlies this system: the MediaPipe team at Google for making production-quality pose estimation accessible on commodity hardware; the Ultralytics team for YOLOv8; and the researchers whose biomechanics studies---Cabarkapa, Okazaki, Struzik, and their colleagues---provided the scientific grounding for our normative ranges.

To our families: thank you for your patience, encouragement, and understanding during the long hours that this project demanded. Your support made it easier to keep going.

Finally, we acknowledge each other. This project required sustained collaboration across two codebases, multiple technology stacks, and a production-readiness audit that would have been impossible to complete individually. Every member of this team contributed ideas, code, debugging effort, and mutual accountability that made SHOOTRZ what it is.
