# 📹 Official Recording Protocol for Accurate Analysis

## 🎯 Purpose

This protocol ensures your videos are recorded correctly for maximum measurement accuracy.

---

## ✅ Step-by-Step Recording Instructions

### **Before Recording:**

1. **Set up camera:**
   - Position: **45° from the side** (not directly front or side)
   - Distance: **10-15 feet** (3-4.5 meters)
   - Height: **Chest level** (camera at shooter's chest height)
   - Framing: Full body visible with space above head and below feet

2. **Check lighting:**
   - Bright, even lighting
   - No shadows on shooter
   - Avoid backlighting (don't face window/sun)

3. **Clear the area:**
   - Uncluttered background
   - No people walking behind
   - Visible orange basketball

### **During Recording:**

1. **Start recording** (phone/camera)

2. **Wait 1-2 seconds** (stand still in ready position)

3. **Perform ONE shot:**
   - Start from set position
   - Perform dip
   - Release shot
   - Hold follow-through for 1 second

4. **Wait 1 second** after follow-through

5. **Stop recording**

**Total video length: 4-8 seconds**

---

## ⚠️ **Critical Requirements:**

### **DO:**
✅ Record ONE shot per video
✅ Start from ready position (not moving)
✅ Include complete motion (setup → follow-through)
✅ Hold phone steady (no panning/moving)
✅ Keep full body in frame throughout
✅ Use good lighting
✅ Record at 30fps or higher
✅ Use 720p or 1080p resolution

### **DON'T:**
❌ Record multiple shots in one video
❌ Start recording mid-shot
❌ Stop recording before follow-through
❌ Move camera during recording
❌ Record from front view (bad for depth)
❌ Record in dim lighting
❌ Cut off head or feet
❌ Record while walking/moving

## ⏱️ Timing Requirements

### **Video Duration:**
- Minimum: 3 seconds (covers shot motion)
- Optimal: 5-7 seconds (includes setup and follow-through)
- Maximum: 10 seconds (system may have trouble finding shot)

### **Shot Timing:**
The system looks for this pattern:
1. **0-2 seconds**: Setup/ready position
2. **2-3 seconds**: Dip motion
3. **3-4 seconds**: Release (peak)
4. **4-5 seconds**: Follow-through
5. **5-6 seconds**: Hold position

If your shot doesn't follow this timing, the system may miss key moments!

## 📱 Phone/Camera Settings

### **Recommended:**
- **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080)
- **Frame Rate:** 30 FPS minimum, 60 FPS ideal
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Orientation:** Landscape (horizontal)
- **Stabilization:** ON
- **Grid:** ON (helps with framing)

### **Phone-Specific:**

**iPhone:**
- Settings → Camera → Record Video → 1080p at 30fps
- Use rear camera
- Lock exposure/focus on shooter

**Android:**
- Settings → Video Quality → FHD (1080p)
- Use rear camera
- Tap to focus on shooter

## 🔍 Pre-Recording Test

### **Before recording your session:**

1. **Record a test shot**
2. **Upload to app**
3. **Check camera analysis:**
   - Reliability should be **80-100%**
   - Angle should show **"45_degree"** or **"side"**
   - Distance should show **"optimal"** or **"close"**

4. **If reliability <80%:**
   - Adjust camera position
   - Move closer/farther
   - Improve lighting
   - Retest

## ⚡ Quick Setup Guide

### **Setup in 60 Seconds:**

1. **Place phone on tripod or stable surface**
   - Or have someone hold it steady

2. **Position 10-12 feet away at 45° angle**
   - Stand to the side-front of shooter
   - Chest height

3. **Frame the shot:**
   - Shooter's head at top 1/4 of frame
   - Feet at bottom 1/4 of frame
   - Some space on sides

4. **Test recording:**
   - Record 2 seconds of standing
   - Check if full body visible
   - Check lighting

5. **Record actual shot:**
   - Stand still 1 sec → Shoot → Hold 1 sec

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### **"Shooting motion not detected"**
→ Video too long, or didn't include complete shot
→ Solution: Record shorter (5-7s), include full motion

### **"Camera angle suboptimal"**
→ Recording from front or too far to side
→ Solution: Move to 45° angle

### **"Low measurement confidence"**
→ Poor lighting, person not fully visible
→ Solution: Better lighting, check framing

### **"Phase detection failed"**
→ Motion pattern unclear
→ Solution: Follow timing protocol (stand still → shoot → hold)
