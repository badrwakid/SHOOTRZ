🌙 Low Brightness (bedside, 10% display)
WATCH
Void (#0A0E14) and primary (#0F141B) compress at very low brightness — the gap closes from 4pt to ~1.5pt perceived. Border hairlines do the heavy lifting. Text tertiary (#64748B) starts to murmur at ≤15% brightness.
Action: ensure tertiary text is never load-bearing (always paired with an icon or primary text nearby). Consider an auto night-boost that nudges text.tertiary to #7A8699 below 20% brightness.
