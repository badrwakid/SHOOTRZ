3 · Clear Space & Minimum Size
1× 1× 1× 1×
clear space1× cap-height of the wordmark on all four sides. Nothing crosses the dashed box — no text, no UI chrome, no other logos.
min · lockupPrimary lockup renders no smaller than 120px / 32mm wide. Below that → switch to logo.small (tagline drops) or logo.icon.
min · iconIcon mark renders no smaller than 16pt / 16px. Below 24pt use the small-size optimized form (seams dropped, S stroke +2pt).
max scaleNo upper limit on the icon. Wordmark retains full detail to 2048px wide; above that export from source SVG rather than upscaling PNG.

5 · Do / Don't
✓Use logo.primary on approved dark surfaces at ≥ 120px wide.
✓Use logo.mono.dark on light surfaces — never full-color on white.
✕Never full-color on orange — wordmark disappears (2.1:1 contrast).
✕Don't skew, stretch, or apply non-uniform scale.
✕Don't recolor. Use an approved mono variant instead.
✕Don't place over unscrimmed photography — use logo.a11y + 60% black scrim.
✕Don't render lockup below 120px wide — swap to logo.small or icon.
Partner
✓Co-brand with 1× clear space divider; partner never larger than SHOOTRZ.
6 · App Icon Crop Guidelines

Android Adaptive · 108×108dp foreground · 72×72dp safe zone · system renders circle / squircle / rounded-square per OEM
Favicon / Web · 32×32 + 16×16 · dropped seams, heavier S stroke · favicon.ico multi-res bundle
